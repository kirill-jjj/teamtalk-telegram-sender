refactor: Remove ActiveTeamTalkConnectionMiddleware

The `ActiveTeamTalkConnectionMiddleware` has been removed.
TeamTalkConnection is now directly injected into handlers and services
using Dishka's dependency injection mechanism, simplifying the architecture
and reducing reliance on middleware for dependency resolution.

This change includes:
- Deletion of `bot/telegram_bot/middlewares/teamtalk_connection.py`.
- Removal of middleware registration in `sender.py` and handler routers.
- Direct injection of `TeamTalkConnection` in relevant handlers.
- Removal of `tt_connection` from `WorkflowData` as it's no longer passed via middleware.
- Removal of `require_tt_connection` and `ensure_tt_user_exists` decorators.
